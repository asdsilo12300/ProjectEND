# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and teachers worldwide who explore plant-science scenarios, run lessons, practise observation skills, compare outcomes, and discuss simulations with classmates. The interface must work for repeated classroom use, independent learning, and multilingual content.

## Product Purpose

Plant Growth Academy is a simulator-led learning product where users grow plants under changing environmental conditions, observe scientifically grounded outcomes, save meaningful game states, and learn socially through shared farms, comments, and community activity. Success means users can understand cause and effect, return to prior simulations, and collaborate without losing the clarity of the core lab experience.

## Brand Personality

Technical, grounded, and quietly playful. It should feel like a polished science-learning game: focused enough for teachers, approachable enough for students, and expressive through the simulation rather than decorative UI.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon gaming interfaces, overly dark green palettes, generic AI-generated dashboards, oversized marketing layouts, decorative card grids, and social feeds that distract from the plant simulation. Avoid controls that imply permissions the user does not have.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the living simulation as the primary visual focus.
2. Make system state, ownership, and available actions unmistakable.
3. Use progressive disclosure so classroom workflows stay compact and scannable.
4. Preserve scientific cause and effect across game, history, and spectator views.
5. Make social activity feel connected to learning outcomes, not separate from them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, complete keyboard support, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives, readable compact typography, and layouts that tolerate longer translated strings. Never rely on color alone to communicate status.
